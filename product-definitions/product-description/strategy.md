## Product Strategy
Gentlii’s strategy is to reduce product professionals’ cognitive load by automating the creation and maintenance of an “opinionated product definition” (also called the “product description”), built by extracting meaning from existing product-related materials. It aims to integrate into existing tools/workflows (be “invisible”), avoid being another UI-heavy app, and continuously guard alignment and validate new ideas/feature requests against the product definition.

## Value Proposition
Gentlii helps product professionals deliver better outcomes by providing a concise, high-quality, trustworthy product definition without requiring additional effort, and by enabling fast alignment checks for ideas/requests—addressing lack of time/headspace for the necessary thinking/planning.

**Confidence:** High

## Strategic Goals
- Reduce cognitive load for product professionals.
- Ensure product work is driven by outcomes (vs output) and has sufficient context before building.
- Produce a concise, reliable set of core product artifacts (“product description”) from existing inputs.
- Maintain/guard alignment across product artifacts and validate new feature requests/ideas against them.
- Integrate into existing tools/workflows; avoid being an additional standalone backlog/agile tool or UI-heavy app.
- Extend/scale product thinking consultancy by making the approach less dependent on consultants.
