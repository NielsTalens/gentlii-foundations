## Product Strategy
Build an “invisible” product that integrates into existing tools/workflows (not a standalone backlog or UI-heavy app) to reduce product professionals’ cognitive load by creating an opinionated, concise, reliable “product definition/product description” from available inputs. The product uses agents to generate core product artifacts (strategy, product vision, business case, product charter) and then “guards” ongoing alignment by validating new ideas/feature requests against that product description and detecting changes in source inputs.

## Value Proposition
Help product professionals deliver better outcomes (not just output) by reducing the time and cognitive effort needed to create high-quality product context/definitions, increasing confidence in the quality of those definitions, and preventing misaligned feature work through fast validation and alignment checks.

## Strategic Goals
- Reduce time to create product description/context by 75%.

- Reduce mis-solution feature builds by 50%.

- Provide stakeholders quick verification of feature request feasibility/alignment.

## Suggestion
- Define the primary target user segments more explicitly (e.g., PMs, product owners, founders, product ops) and the initial wedge use case.
- Clarify how “quality” of the product description will be measured and what “confidently right on all facets” means (metrics, evaluation method, baselines).
- Specify the intended integrations (“existing tools and workflows”) for the first releases (e.g., Jira, Confluence, Notion, Slack, Google Drive).
- Add a clear long-term product vision/positioning statement (e.g., “system of record for product context” vs “copilot for product strategy”).
- Describe the change-detection/guarding workflow end-to-end (what triggers, what outputs, who approves, and how conflicts are resolved).
