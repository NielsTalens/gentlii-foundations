## Product Strategy

Gentlii focuses on digital product development by reducing the cognitive load of product professionals through an “opinionated product definition” / “product description” that is automatically created from existing product inputs, kept aligned, and used to validate new ideas and feature requests. The product should be largely “invisible” by integrating into existing tools and workflows (not “another app” with accounts/UI), and should prioritize outcomes over output (countering spec-driven and output-focused trends).
