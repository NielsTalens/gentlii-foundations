## Strategic Pillars
- **Reduce cognitive load via invisibility/integration:** avoid being “another app” and integrate into existing tools/workflows; “make the product invisible” where possible.  
- **Opinionated, concise product definition with trusted quality:** produce a concise product definition users can “trust” without extra effort.  
- **Outcome-first product thinking:** prioritize defining outcomes over generating output.  
- **Automated extraction + continuous guarding:** generate key product artifacts from “all available data,” and rerun guarding on input changes (CRUD detection).  
- **Alignment & feature validation:** keep artifacts aligned and validate feature requests against the product description.

## Suggestion
- Define the **primary buyer/user persona** more precisely (e.g., PM, PO, product lead, startup founder) and the context (enterprise vs SMB, regulated vs not).  
- Make the “**quality**” metric measurable (e.g., rubric dimensions, evaluation method, target score, who validates, timeframe).  
- Clarify the intended **distribution/integration points** (“existing tools and workflows”) with examples (e.g., Jira/Confluence/Notion/GitHub/Slack) and what “invisible” means in practice.  
- Specify the **core strategic bet** versus alternatives (e.g., why “opinionated product definition” is the winning approach vs flexible templates).  
- Add leading indicators for success (e.g., adoption/retention of the product description, % feature requests validated, time-to-decision, stakeholder satisfaction).
