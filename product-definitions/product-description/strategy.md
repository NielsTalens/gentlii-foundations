## Company Strategy

Build a new kind of CRM positioned explicitly against traditional CRMs (e.g., Salesforce/HubSpot) by removing dashboards, manual data entry, and heavy configuration, and instead delivering a conversation-first, automation-by-default experience that drives daily usage and reduces “CRM fatigue.” Initial go-to-market focus is narrow (“not trying to boil the ocean”), targeting sales reps and founders doing sales, with potential expansion to customer success later.

## Product Strategy

Develop a conversation-first CRM (chat-first; voice possibly later) with minimal/no traditional screens, centered on “next best action” and rapid execution. The product should proactively suggest prioritized actions, automate capture/logging (emails/calls), and keep the pipeline updated invisibly in the background, with design principles emphasizing actions over insights, reduced choices, and a calm/direct tone. Avoid building reporting/BI, dashboards, and manual pipeline management—treat any drift toward tabs/dashboards as losing direction.

**Confidence:** High

**Evidence:** “It’s basically a CRM but conversation-first. Probably chat based, maybe voice later. ## Value Proposition

A CRM that removes admin work and fragmentation by telling users exactly what to do next (prioritized), executing actions quickly, and automatically logging/updating everything—so users feel calm and in control rather than overwhelmed, and can focus on selling instead of maintaining a system.

**Confidence:** High

## Strategic Goals

Reduce CRM fatigue/stress and the feeling of admin work; drive daily active usage by reps; increase revenue per rep via better follow-up; make onboarding nonexistent or very fast (needing training indicates failure); achieve “basically zero data entry” with automated capture and background pipeline updates.

## Long-term Direction

Move toward an “invisible” CRM that proactively guides work: the system suggests actions (ideally before the user asks), updates itself in the background, and stays out of the way—pushing conversation-first and no-screens as far as feasible.
