## Strategic Pillars
- Opinionated, concise “product description” as the core artifact (strategy, product vision, business case, product charter).
- Automation from existing inputs: ingest “all types of files” and extract meaning to generate the product description (markdown + HTML).
- “Invisible” integration: avoid a standalone app/UI; integrate into existing tools/workflows to minimize user effort.
- Continuous alignment guarding: detect changes in inputs and produce a guard report to keep artifacts consistent.
- Feature validation against context: score/rate feature ideas against the product description (outcome over output).
