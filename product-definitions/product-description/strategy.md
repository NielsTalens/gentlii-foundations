## Strategic Pillars
- **Reduce cognitive load via minimal/invisible UX:** avoid “another app” and UI/account burden; integrate into existing tools/workflows.  
- **Opinionated, high-quality product definition:** gather necessary info and produce a concise product definition users can “trust.”  
- **Outcome-over-output orientation:** emphasize outcome definition and product quality vs generating more output/specs.  
- **Alignment & validation guardrails:** check ideas/feature requests against the product definition; guard consistency across documents.  
- **Data-driven extraction from existing artifacts:** ingest/upload “all types of files” and extract meaning to generate core documents.

## Suggestion
- Define the intended deployment/integration targets explicitly (e.g., which “existing tools and workflows”) to make the “invisible” pillar actionable.  
- Turn “quality… confidently right on all facets” into concrete, testable quality metrics (e.g., completeness/consistency checks, stakeholder approval rate, rework rate).  
- Clarify the operating model: self-serve product vs consultancy-assisted onboarding/implementation (and how the extension of consultancy works in practice).  
- Specify how “alignment guarding” works over time (cadence, triggers, approvals) beyond “crud detected in product input files.”  
- Add explicit customer segments/roles (PM, PO, Head of Product, etc.) and primary use cases per segment to sharpen go-to-market focus.
