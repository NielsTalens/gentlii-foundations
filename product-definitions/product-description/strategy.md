## Product Strategy
Gentlii’s strategy is to reduce product professionals’ cognitive load by capturing needed information into an “opinionated product definition” (a set of strategic/product documents), ensuring its quality without user effort, and continuously checking/guarding alignment between ideas, feature requests, and the product definition. It should avoid becoming a standalone backlog/agile tool or an extra UI-heavy app; instead, it should integrate into existing tools/workflows and be “invisible” where possible. It generates core artifacts (strategy, product vision, business case, product charter) by extracting meaning from available data, and then validates new requests against that description.

## Value Proposition
Gentlii helps product professionals deliver higher-quality product development outcomes by doing the heavy thinking/planning work they lack time/headspace for: automatically producing a concise, reliable product definition from existing materials and providing ongoing alignment checks so teams don’t “start building too early without enough product context.”

- Reduce cognitive load for product professionals by avoiding a new standalone UI/app and integrating into existing workflows.  
- Create a concise “product description” (strategy, product vision, business case, product charter) by extracting meaning from available data/files.  
- Ensure users can rely on product-definition quality “without any required action.”

## Suggestion
- Define explicit success metrics (e.g., time saved, alignment/decision-cycle improvements, reduction in rework) tied to the “outcomes over output” principle.  
- Clarify target users and primary use cases (e.g., PMs in scale-ups vs enterprise product orgs; new product vs existing product).  
- Specify the integration surface area (which “existing tools and workflows” first) and an MVP scope for “invisible” usage.  
- State how “quality… without any required action” will be achieved/verified (quality criteria, confidence scores, human review options).  
- Make the long-term positioning clearer: SaaS product, consultancy-adjacent offering, or hybrid (and how it scales beyond bespoke inputs).
