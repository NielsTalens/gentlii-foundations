## User Flows or Journeys
### Morning / starting point
- Current: user opens multiple tools (CRM/email/calendar/Slack) and figures out what to do first.  
- Desired: system immediately tells them what to do (“contact X”, “follow up with Y”, “deal Z is at risk”), prioritized.

**Confidence:** High  
**Evidence:** “starting point (morning / opening laptop)… first question… what do I do first”; “today they usually check like 3–5 things… fragmented”; “system just tells them immediately… ideally ranked / prioritized.”

### Figuring out next action (current vs desired)

- Desired: ask (or be told) “what should I do now?”, receive prioritized actions with who/context/suggested message/next step.

**Confidence:** High

**Confidence:** High
