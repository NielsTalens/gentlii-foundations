business case

Completeness: Complete

Strength: Medium

Business Rationale

Agile methods alone are not delivering faster value; achieving faster value delivery requires more upfront thinking, planning, and context-building than most product professionals have time/space for. Industry trends (spec-driven development and “output over outcome”) reinforce building too early and focusing on output instead of product quality and outcomes. Gentlii exists to reduce cognitive load and provide reliable, high-quality product definition/context to enable better product development decisions.

Confidence: High

Evidence

“the agile promise of faster value delivery sadly does not come from the processes like scrum,” “it requires much more thinking and planning,” “most people do not have the time nor the head space,” “start building too early without enough product context,” “Output over outcome,” “This product is about reducing cognitive load of product professionals.”

Contradictions

No contradictory content found.

Expected Value

Creates higher-quality product context/definition (strategy, vision, charter, business case) from existing data, with less effort from product professionals; ensures ongoing alignment of ideas/features to that definition; reduces unnecessary feature build and improves product quality (better features with less code), saving time and money and improving clarity/feedback for stakeholders.

Confidence: High

Evidence

“providing help in gathering the needed information for solid product development… opinionated product definition,” “making sure they can rely on the quality… without any required action,” “check whether any idea aligns,” “create strategic, product vision, product charter documents… extracting the meaning from all available data,” “guard alignment,” “validate feature requests… give clarity and quick feedback,” “time and money saved,” “Better features with less code.”

Gentlii can extract sufficient meaning from “all available data” (uploaded files / existing artifacts) to generate a “confidently right” product description; integrating into existing tools/workflows (and being “invisible”) will reduce cognitive load rather than adding overhead; validating feature requests against the product description will meaningfully prevent wrong features from being built; quality of product definition can be measured and improved “backed up by data.”

  • Improve “quality of the product description” to a level “confidently right on all facets” (intended to be backed by data; metric not fully specified).
