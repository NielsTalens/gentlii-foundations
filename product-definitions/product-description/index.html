Product Strategy

Build an “invisible” product that integrates into existing tools/workflows (not a standalone backlog or UI-heavy app) to reduce product professionals’ cognitive load by creating an opinionated, concise, reliable “product definition/product description” from available inputs. The product uses agents to generate core product artifacts (strategy, product vision, business case, product charter) and then “guards” ongoing alignment by validating new ideas/feature requests against that product description and detecting changes in source inputs.

Confidence: High

Evidence

Business Rationale

Gentlii is positioned to address the gap between “agile promise” and actual faster value delivery by helping product professionals do the additional thinking/planning required for solid product development, despite limited time/headspace and environmental constraints. It also responds to industry trends of spec-driven development and output-over-outcome by shifting focus to product context and outcomes. Additionally, it is framed as an extension to existing product thinking consultancy that is currently too dependent on consultants “pulling the process.”
