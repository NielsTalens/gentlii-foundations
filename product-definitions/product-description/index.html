business case

Completeness: Complete

Strength: Medium

Business Rationale

Gentlii exists to address a gap in agile/product development where “faster value delivery” does not come from processes alone, but from better upfront thinking, planning, and product context—something many product professionals lack time and environment for. It also positions itself against industry trends of spec-driven development and “output over outcome,” arguing teams start building too early without enough context. Additionally, it is framed as an extension/scaling of existing product thinking consultancy that is currently too dependent on consultants.

Confidence: High

Evidence

“the agile promise of faster value delivery sadly does not come from the processes like scrum”; “it requires much more thinking and planning and making smart choices”; “most people do not have the time nor the head space”; “start building too early without enough product context”; “Output over outcome”; “Gentlii could be a extension of the product thinking consultancy work… embedded in the consultant pulling the process.”
