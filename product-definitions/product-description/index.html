Company Strategy

Build a differentiated CRM company by positioning against legacy CRMs (e.g., Salesforce/HubSpot) as “broken” for day-to-day sellers, and focusing on a narrow initial segment (sales reps and founders) rather than broad, enterprise-style feature breadth (“not trying to boil the ocean”). The strategy emphasizes adoption through reduced friction (minimal training, daily usage), and differentiation via conversation-first interaction, automation, and action-orientation (not analytics/reporting).

Product Strategy

Create a conversation-first CRM (chat as primary UI; voice possibly later) that minimizes or removes traditional screens/tabs/dashboards and instead provides prioritized “next best actions,” executes actions quickly, and automates data capture and pipeline updates in the background (“invisible” CRM). User Flows or Journeys

Morning start: user opens app (ideally minimal/no login) and immediately gets prioritized actions (“contact X”, “follow up with Y”, “deal Z is at risk”) instead of checking multiple tools. Next-action discovery (current vs desired): current involves browsing CRM, dashboards, notes/emails and manually deciding; desired is asking (or being proactively told) “what should I do now?” and receiving a prioritized action list with who/context/suggested next step. Executing an action (current vs desired): current is find contact → read notes → switch to email → write → maybe log → update deal stage; desired is respond in chat (“send this/call now/schedule”) → action executes → auto-logging + background pipeline update with no “after work.” Capturing activity: current requires logging notes/updates and is often skipped; desired is automatic email/call capture, suggested/generated notes, and automatic deal stage updates (“basically zero data entry”). Staying on top of deals: current relies on static dashboards and interpretation; desired is proactive flags (“deal is going cold”, “you haven’t replied in 3 days”) converted into executable actions.
