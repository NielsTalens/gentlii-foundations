business case

Completeness

Complete

Strength

Medium

Business Rationale

Gentlii exists to address a gap in agile/product development: faster value delivery requires more upfront thinking, planning, and product context than most product professionals have time/headspace for, and current trends (spec-driven, output-focused AI) push teams to build too early without enough context or focus on outcomes. Gentlii is also positioned as an extension of existing product thinking consultancy, reducing dependence on consultants “pulling the process.”

Confidence: High

Evidence

“the agile promise of faster value delivery does not come from the processes like scrum”; “it requires much more thinking and planning”; “most people do not have the time nor the head space”; “start building too early without enough product context”; “Output over outcome”; “Gentlii could be a extension of the product thinking consultancy work… too much embedded in the consultant pulling the process.”

Contradictions:

No contradictory content found.

Expected Value

Reduced cognitive load for product professionals by automating/assisting creation and maintenance of a concise, trusted product definition (“product description”) and ensuring ongoing alignment of ideas/feature requests with that definition. Value also includes faster/clearer stakeholder feedback and reduced waste (building wrong features), leading to time and cost savings and better product quality.

Confidence: High

Evidence

“This product is about reducing cognitive load of product professionals”; “providing help in gathering the needed information for solid product development”; “rely on the quality of this product definition without any required action”; “check whether any idea aligns with the product definition”; “provide stakeholders a way to quickly verify a feature requests possibility → give clarity and quick feedback”; “reduce the amount of features that will be build that are not the right solution.”

Contradictions:

No contradictory content found.

Assumptions

  • Sufficient relevant “available data” (files, existing artifacts, inputs) can be collected/uploaded to accurately extract meaning and generate strategy/vision/charter and related artifacts.
  • Users will trust an “opinionated” product definition that requires “no required action” to ensure quality.
  • Integrating into existing tools/workflows (and being “invisible,” not an additional UI/app) is feasible and will reduce cognitive load rather than add friction.
  • Validating feature requests against the product description will reduce misaligned work and improve outcomes.

Confidence: Medium

Evidence

“A user uploads all types of files they can find that state something about the product”; “created by extracting the meaning from all available data”; “opinionated product definition”; “rely on the quality… without any required action”; “should not be an additional app… integrate within existing tools and workflows”; “validate feature requests against the product description.”

Contradictions:

Potential tension between “without any required action” and “A user uploads all types of files…” (some user effort is required).

Measurable Business Outcomes

  • Reduce time to create a proper product description/context by 75%.
  • Improve quality of product description to a level that is “confidently right on all facets” (claimed to be data-backed).
  • Reduce number of wrong-solution features built by 50% (tracked via feature requests marked “Not good”).
  • Save ~25% in operations/bugs/other costs by improving product with less code (“Better features with less code”).

Confidence: High

Evidence

“Reduce the time to create a proper product description/context by 75%”; “prove (bakced up by data) that the quality… will improve”; “reduce the amount of features… not the right solution by 50% (we can register how many feature requests are marked Not good)”; “save 25% op operations, bugs and other expensive stuff.”

Contradictions:

No contradictory content found.

Suggestion

  • Define how “quality of the product description… confidently right on all facets” will be measured (e.g., scoring rubric, validation method, target thresholds).
  • Clarify data/inputs assumptions: what minimum set of artifacts is required and what happens when uploaded data is incomplete/contradictory.
  • Tighten the causal chain from “alignment guarding/validation” → “fewer wrong features” → “cost/time savings,” including baseline definitions for each metric.
  • Specify adoption/integration success measures (since the product should be “invisible” and integrated into existing tools).
