Business Rationale

Gentlii exists to address a gap in digital product development: “agile” practices alone don’t deliver faster value without deeper product thinking, but product professionals often lack time/headspace and supportive environments to do that thinking. It also responds to industry trends that over-emphasize spec-driven development and AI output over product quality/outcomes, leading teams to “start building too early without enough product context.” Additionally, it can productize/extend existing product-thinking consultancy work that is currently too dependent on consultants.

Confidence: High

Evidence

“The problem is that the agile promise of faster value delivery does not come from the processes like scrum… it requires much more thinking and planning…”; “most people do not have the time nor the head space…”; “start building too early without enough product context.”; “Output over outcome…”; “Gentlii could be a extension of the product thinking consultancy work.”

No contradictory content found.

Expected Value

Gentlii is expected to reduce cognitive load for product professionals, improve the quality and reliability of product definitions/context, increase alignment of ideas/features to strategy/vision/charter, provide faster stakeholder clarity/feedback on feature requests, and reduce waste (building wrong features) and downstream operational costs (less code, fewer bugs/expensive issues). It also aims to integrate into existing tools/workflows rather than adding UI/account overhead.
