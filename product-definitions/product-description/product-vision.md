Gentlii aims to reduce the cognitive load on product professionals by creating a trustworthy, concise product definition (product description) from existing product inputs, and using it to keep product decisions aligned toward outcomes (not output).

## Product Features
- Ingest/upload “all types of files” and “all available data” related to a product/idea.
- Automatically generate an opinionated product definition (“product description”) including: Strategy, Product Vision, Business case, Product charter.
- Provide high-confidence quality/reliability of the generated product definition with minimal/no user action.
- Guard alignment/consistency across the generated output documents.
- Validate feature requests/ideas against the product description and give quick clarity/feedback on feasibility/alignment.
- Integrate into existing tools/workflows and remain “invisible” where possible (not a separate backlog/agile tool).

## Suggestion

- Clarify the primary target persona(s) within “product professionals” (e.g., PMs, product ops, founders) and the secondary audiences (stakeholders, leadership).
