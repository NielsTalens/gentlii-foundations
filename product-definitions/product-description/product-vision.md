## Vision Statement
Replace complex CRM software with a conversational system that tells users exactly what to do next, shifting CRM from a system-of-record to a system-of-action/execution and becoming “invisible” over time.

## Needs
- Reduce CRM admin burden: heavy manual entry, field updates, pipeline upkeep
- Eliminate complex navigation and dashboards; reduce “clicking around”
- Provide clear, prioritized “next best action” to remove decision fatigue
- Reduce cognitive load and feelings of overwhelm/stress/guilt from being “behind”
- Prevent fragmented workflows across CRM/email/calendar/Slack and shadow systems (spreadsheets/notes/WhatsApp)
- Improve data reliability by ensuring activities are logged and pipeline stays accurate

## Product Features
- Conversation-first interaction as primary mode (chat; possibly voice later)
- “Next best action” recommendations, ideally proactive and prioritized
- Fast execution of actions (send follow-up, call, schedule) from the conversational flow
- Automatic capture/logging of sales activity (emails, calls), with suggested/generated notes
- Background pipeline maintenance (deal stage updates without manual input)
- Deal risk/attention flags converted into executable actions (not analytics/reporting)
- Minimal choices/low configuration approach to keep the product focused on action

High

## Differentiators

- One primary interface (chat) and one primary output (next action); minimal/no traditional screens
- Automation-by-default: “zero data entry” with background updates
- Anti-dashboard/anti-reporting stance: actions over insights/charts
- Product “tone” and behavior: focused, direct, decisive; avoids hype and clutter
