## Vision Statement

Replace complex CRM software with a conversational system that tells you exactly what to do next, shifting CRM from a “system of record” to a “system of action/execution” with minimal cognitive load.

## Needs

- Reduce manual data entry and admin overhead (logging, updating fields/stages).
- Provide clear “next best action”/prioritized guidance on what to do next.
- Reduce cognitive load, choices, and navigation complexity.
- Keep pipeline accurate and trusted (reduce inaccurate pipeline and shadow systems).
- Enable faster execution with less context switching (CRM/email/calendar).
- Emotional need: feel calm/in control; reduce overwhelm, stress, and guilt from an “always behind” CRM.

## Product Features

- Conversation-first CRM interaction (chat-based; potentially voice input later).
- “Next best action” recommendations (prioritized list; ideally suggested proactively).
- Automatic activity capture/logging (emails, calls; notes generated/suggested).
- Automatic pipeline updates in the background (e.g., deal stage updates without manual input).
- Fast execution of actions from recommendations (send follow-up, schedule, call).
- Risk/cold-deal detection translated into executable actions (flags tied to actions).
- Minimal/avoid traditional screens, dashboards, reporting.

- Focus on action/execution rather than dashboards/analytics/BI (“system-of-action”).
- Automation by default: capture/log/update pipeline invisibly with near-zero data entry.
- Decisive, direct guidance (“tells you what to do next”), reducing searching and deciding.

## Suggestion

- Resolve the “no dashboards/actions > insights” principle vs customer demand for “more insights/custom dashboards” by defining a constrained “insights-to-actions” approach (e.g., explanations and flags that always end in an executable next step).
- Clarify the initial ICP and rollout sequence (sales reps vs founders) and explicitly state what is out of scope for v1 (e.g., reporting, customization).
- Tighten the vision into a single concise statement used consistently across docs (system-of-action + invisible automation + next best action).
- Specify 3–5 core outcomes you will optimize for (adoption, time saved, follow-up rate, pipeline accuracy) even if exact metrics are TBD.
