## Needs

Users find existing CRMs “broken” because they require too much clicking, manual updating, and dashboard-checking, which feels like admin work rather than helping them sell. They want clear guidance on what to do next, fast interaction, reduced fragmentation across tools (CRM/email/calendar), and near-zero data entry. There’s also an emotional need: feeling in control, not overwhelmed, and reducing “stress + guilt” from an always-behind CRM.

**Confidence:** High

**Evidence:** “CRM as it exists today… is kind of broken… It’s too much clicking around, updating fields, checking dashboards… feels like admin work”; “they want to know what to do next, clearly”; “they don’t want to manually update anything”; “everything to be fast (input + output)”; “today they usually check like 3–5 things (CRM, email, calendar, maybe Slack) → already fragmented”; “basically zero data entry”; “users don’t just want efficiency… to feel in control… not feel overwhelmed”; “right now CRM = stress + guilt”

**Contradictions:** No contradictory content found.

## Product Features

A conversation-first CRM (chat-based, with possible voice later) that provides “next best action” guidance—ideally proactively—and prioritizes actions so the user doesn’t need to browse dashboards or pipelines. It should automatically capture/log activity (emails, calls), generate or suggest notes, update the pipeline/deal stage in the background, flag at-risk/cooling deals and translate those into executable actions, and enable quick execution of tasks (send follow-ups, call, schedule) directly from the conversational interface.

**Confidence:** High

**Evidence:** “CRM but conversation-first. Probably chat based, maybe voice later. No real ‘screens’”; “tell you your next best action”; “log stuff automatically (calls, emails etc.)”; “keep the pipeline updated without you touching it”; “let you execute things quickly (send follow-up, schedule, etc.)”; “system suggests actions before you ask (ideally)”; “get list of actions already prioritized”; “each item includes: who, context, suggested message or next step”; “notes generated or at least suggested”; “deal stage updated without asking user”

**Contradictions:** No contradictory content found.

## Business Goals

Reduce CRM fatigue, drive daily usage/adoption by reps, increase revenue per rep (via better follow-up), and make onboarding/training minimal to nonexistent.

**Confidence:** High

**Evidence:** “Main goals are: reduce the general fatigue people have with CRM”; “get reps to actually use the system daily”; “increase revenue per rep (if they follow up more, this should happen)”; “make onboarding basically nonexistent or very fast”; “If someone needs training, we probably failed.”

**Contradictions:** No contradictory content found.

## Differentiators

Positioned as “not another version of Salesforce”: conversation-first with minimal/no traditional screens, tabs, dashboards, reporting/BI, and no manual pipeline management or heavy configurability. Emphasizes actions over insights (less viewing/more doing), automation by default with background updates, decisive “tell you what to do” behavior, and a calm/direct tone (short explanations instead of reports).

**Confidence:** High

**Evidence:** “conversation first, always… everything through chat / language, no screens if we can avoid it”; “actions > insights… avoid anything that looks like reporting”; “automation by default… if user has to fill fields → something went wrong”; “not a reporting tool… not BI… basically not another version of Salesforce”; “We’re intentionally not doing: traditional screens; analytics dashboards… manual pipeline management”; “Everything is more like: → action → next step → done”; “product should feel… calm… a bit decisive (almost telling you what to do)”; “if you ask ‘why this?’ → short explanation, not a report”

**Contradictions:** Minor tension: the vision says “no real ‘screens’” / “no screens if we can avoid it,” but also allows for partial screens and later voice as possibilities rather than absolutes.

**Evidence:** “No real ‘screens’ in the traditional sense, or at least that’s the idea right now.”; “no screens if we can avoid it”; “Probably chat based, maybe voice later.”
