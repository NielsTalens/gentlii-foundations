## Product Features
- Ingest/upload existing product-related files/data as input
- Automatically extract meaning from available data to generate core product artifacts (strategy, product vision, business case, product charter) as a concise “product description”
- Provide an opinionated product definition and ensure users can rely on its quality “without any required action”
- Guard/monitor product description alignment and rerun checks when inputs change, producing a report
- Validate/rate feature requests against the product description to assess fit/quality of idea
- Integrate into existing tools and workflows (avoid being a standalone app)
