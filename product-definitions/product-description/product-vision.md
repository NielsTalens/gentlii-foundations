## Product Features
- Conversation-first CRM (chat-based; potential voice later) with minimal/no traditional screens/tabs/dashboards  
- “What should I do now?” next-best-action guidance (ideally proactive suggestions), prioritized action list with context and suggested message/next step  
- Quick execution of actions from chat (send follow-up, call, schedule)  
- Automation by default: automatic capture/logging of emails/calls; background pipeline/deal-stage updates; notes generated or suggested  
- Deal risk/cold alerts converted into executable actions (not reporting)  
- **Confidence: High**  
- **Evidence:**

  - “ability to just ask ‘what should I do now?’” / “system suggests actions before you ask (ideally)”
