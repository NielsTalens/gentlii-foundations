## Business Rationale
Gentlii is positioned as a response to the gap between “agile” processes and actually delivering value faster: effective delivery requires more upfront thinking/planning and product context, but product professionals lack time/headspace and their environment doesn’t support it. The product aims to reduce cognitive load and prevent building too early by creating and maintaining an “opinionated product definition/product description” from existing materials, and using it to check alignment of ideas/features.
