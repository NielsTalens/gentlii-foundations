## Business Rationale

Agile methods alone are not delivering faster value; achieving faster value delivery requires more upfront thinking, planning, and context-building than most product professionals have time/space for. Industry trends (spec-driven development and “output over outcome”) reinforce building too early and focusing on output instead of product quality and outcomes. Gentlii exists to reduce cognitive load and provide reliable, high-quality product definition/context to enable better product development decisions.

## Suggestion
