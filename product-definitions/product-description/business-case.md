## Business Rationale

Gentlii exists to address a gap in digital product development: “agile” practices alone don’t deliver faster value without deeper product thinking, but product professionals often lack time/headspace and supportive environments to do that thinking. It also responds to industry trends that over-emphasize spec-driven development and AI output over product quality/outcomes, leading teams to “start building too early without enough product context.” Additionally, it can productize/extend existing product-thinking consultancy work that is currently too dependent on consultants.

**Evidence:** “The problem is that the agile promise of faster value delivery does not come from the processes like scrum… it requires much more thinking and planning…”; “most people do not have the time nor the head space…”; “start building too early without enough product context.”; “Output over outcome…”; “Gentlii could be a extension of the product thinking consultancy work.”

## Expected Value

Gentlii is expected to reduce cognitive load for product professionals, improve the quality and reliability of product definitions/context, increase alignment of ideas/features to strategy/vision/charter, provide faster stakeholder clarity/feedback on feature requests, and reduce waste (building wrong features) and downstream operational costs (less code, fewer bugs/expensive issues). It also aims to integrate into existing tools/workflows rather than adding UI/account overhead.

**Confidence:** High

**Evidence:** “This product is about reducing cognitive load of product professionals.”; “providing help in gathering the needed information for solid product development… opinionated product definition.”; “rely on the quality of this product definition without any required action”; “check whether any idea aligns with the product definition”; “integrate within existing tools and workflows.”; “reduce the amount of features that will be build that are not the right solution… time and money saved.”; “Better features with less code save… operations, bugs…”

## Assumptions

- Automatically extracting meaning from “all available data” and uploaded files can produce strategic/product documents that are “confidently right on all facets.”
- Users will have sufficient and relevant source materials to upload (“all types of files they can find”) and those materials reflect true product intent/context.
- Integration into existing workflows/tools is feasible while still delivering the needed capability (creation + guarding + validation) without becoming “an additional app with an UI… account.”
- Feature request validation and labeling (“Not good”) is a reliable proxy to quantify reduction of wrong features.

## Suggestion

- Define how “quality of the product description… confidently right on all facets” will be measured (e.g., rubric, stakeholder acceptance rate, defect rate in downstream decisions).
- Add explicit metrics for “quick feedback”/stakeholder verification (e.g., time-to-answer on feature feasibility, % requests resolved within X hours).
- Make key assumptions testable (e.g., minimum viable input set, data freshness, required document types, integration constraints).
- Clarify the causal chain from “product description guarding/validation” to the 50% reduction in wrong features and 25% ops/bugs savings (what mechanisms drive the savings).
- Specify baseline definitions for the percentage targets (75/50/25%) and how/where they will be captured in real workflows.
