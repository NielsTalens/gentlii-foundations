## Business Rationale

Gentlii exists to address a gap where “agile” and spec-driven practices do not reliably lead to faster value delivery because teams start building too early without sufficient product context, and product professionals lack the time/headspace/environment to do the necessary upfront thinking and planning.

**Confidence:** High

**Evidence:** “the agile promise of faster value delivery does not come from the processes like scrum”; “it requires much more thinking and planning”; “most people do not have the time nor the head space”; “start building too early without enough product context.”

**Contradictions:** No contradictory content found.

## Expected Value

Gentlii is expected to reduce cognitive load for product professionals by automating creation of a concise, trusted “product definition/description” and maintaining alignment/validation of ideas and feature requests against it—shifting focus from output to outcomes and improving product quality and decision-making.

**Confidence:** High

**Evidence:** “This product is about reducing cognitive load of product professionals”; “providing help in gathering the needed information for solid product development… an opinionated product definition”; “rely on the quality… without any required action”; “check whether any idea aligns with the product definition”; “improve the quality of the products. Output over outcome…”

**Contradictions:** No contradictory content found.

## Assumptions

- Users can provide sufficient “available data” (files/inputs) for the system to extract meaning and generate strategy/vision/charter/business case artifacts.
- The generated product definition can be trusted “without any required action” by users (i.e., automation can achieve reliable quality).
- Integration into existing tools/workflows (not “another app”) is feasible and will reduce cognitive load rather than add friction.
- Validation/alignment checks will be adopted by stakeholders to verify feature requests and influence build decisions.

**Confidence:** Medium

**Evidence:** “A user uploads all types of files they can find”; “created by extracting the meaning from all available data”; “rely on the quality… without any required action”; “should integrate within existing tools and workflows”; “provide stakeholders a way to quickly verify a feature requests possibility.”

**Contradictions:** Potential tension between “quality… confidently right on all facets” and “without any required action,” but no explicit contradiction stated.

## Measurable Business Outcomes

- Reduce time to create a proper product description/context by 75%.
- Reduce “features that will be build that are not the right solution” by 50% (tracked via feature requests marked “Not good”), saving time and money.
- Improve operations/bugs/“expensive stuff” by enabling “better features with less code,” saving 25%.
- Improve product description quality “to a state where it is confidently right on all facets” (claimed to be “backed up by data,” but no specific metric defined).
