## Business Rationale
Gentlii is positioned as a response to the gap between “agile” process adoption and actually delivering value faster: teams need more thinking, planning, and context-building than they have time/headspace for, and industry trends over-focus on specs and AI-generated output rather than outcomes/quality. The product also connects to an existing “product thinking consultancy” model, aiming to reduce dependency on consultants by embedding the process into a product.
