## Business Rationale

Gentlii exists because “agile” processes alone are not delivering faster value; delivering value requires deeper product thinking, planning, and smart choices that many product professionals lack time/space to do in their environments. The industry is also trending toward spec-driven development and AI output volume, which (per the text) leads teams to build too early and optimize output over outcomes. Gentlii is positioned as an extension of existing product-thinking consultancy, reducing dependency on consultants to drive the process.

## Suggestion

- Define how “quality of the product description” will be measured (rubric, reviewers, error rates, alignment scores) and what “confidently right on all facets” means operationally.
- Specify baseline measurements and time horizons for the 75%/50%/25% targets (e.g., current cycle time, current misfit-feature rate, period of measurement).
- Make assumptions testable: minimum viable input set (“all types of files”) and what happens when inputs are incomplete/contradictory.
- Add explicit business impact linkage (e.g., how reduced misfit features converts to cost savings, cycle-time reduction, or revenue impact) and who owns those metrics.
- Clarify adoption constraints: which “existing tools and workflows” are in scope for integration and how “invisible” usage will be achieved/measured.
